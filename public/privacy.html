Privacy Policy

Last updated September 21, 2026

LUX Video OS ("LUX," "we," "us") is an AI video studio: you provide either your own photo and a recorded narration (Standard) or a script and a licensed presenter selection (Premium), and we render a video for your account. This policy explains what we collect to do that, how we use it, and who else ever sees it.

Information we collect

Account information. The email address and name you sign in with — via Google, a magic-link email, or a workspace username/password — and a record of when you sign in.

Content you provide. For Standard renders: the portrait photo and WAV narration recording you upload, and your explicit consent confirmation for that specific photo-and-recording pair. For Premium renders: the script you write (or draft with the optional AI copywriter) and your chosen licensed presenter and voice — Premium presenters are licensed by LUX, not your own likeness. For both: the finished video itself, stored privately to your account.

Payment information. Purchases are handled directly by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number — only what Stripe returns to confirm a purchase (amount, credits granted, transaction status).

Technical information. A session cookie that keeps you signed in, and standard operational logs (timestamps, error reports) used to keep the service running and to diagnose problems. Error reports are scrubbed of session tokens and authorization headers before they ever leave our systems.

We do not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics on this site.

How we use your information

  • To render the specific video you request, and to let you review, download, and manage it.
  • To operate your account: sign-in, credit balance, purchase history, and support requests.
  • To detect and prevent abuse, and to keep the service secure and reliable.
  • To send you service email: render-ready notifications, sign-in links, and purchase receipts.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use your photo, voice, or video content to train any AI model — ours or anyone else's — beyond generating the specific render you asked for.

Who we share information with

We use a small number of service providers to operate LUX, each of which only receives what it needs to do its job:

  • Google — if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Resend — to deliver sign-in and notification email.
  • Stripe — to process credit-pack purchases.
  • HeyGen — to render Premium-tier videos (receives your script and presenter/voice selection, not your own photo or voice).
  • RunPod — to render Standard-tier videos (receives your uploaded portrait and narration, solely to generate that render).
  • Vercel and Neon — our hosting, storage, and database infrastructure.
  • Sentry — sanitized error monitoring, with authentication data removed before we receive it.

We do not share your information with any other party for their own marketing or advertising purposes.

Your consent for your photo and voice

Every Standard-tier render requires you to explicitly confirm, at submission time, that you're authorized to use the specific portrait and recording you're uploading. That consent is recorded and tied to that render — it is not a blanket, ongoing authorization, and it is never reused for a different video without asking again.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account, uploaded content, and rendered videos for as long as your account is active, so you can keep coming back to your work. To request deletion of your account and associated data, email ariel@luxmarketingcompany.com — we don't currently have a self-service delete button, so this goes to a real person.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain uses of it. Contact us at the address above and we'll act on it; we may need to verify it's really your account first.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards — encrypted connections, access-controlled storage, and least-privilege access to production systems — but no service can guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password (or sign in with Google) and let us know right away if you notice anything suspicious on your account.

Children's privacy

LUX Video OS is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, let account holders know by email.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email ariel@luxmarketingcompany.com.
