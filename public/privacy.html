Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

LUX Video OS ("LUX," "we," "us") is an AI video studio. You can provide a portrait and recorded narration for a Standard video, or use an authorized presenter and voice with a script for a scripted Standard or Premium video. This policy explains the information we use to create identities and videos, and the providers that may process it.

Information we collect

Account information. The email address and name you sign in with — via Google, a magic-link email, or a workspace username/password — and a record of when you sign in.

Content you provide. This can include portrait photos, source recordings, narration audio, scripts, descriptions used to generate a presenter, presenter and voice selections, and finished videos. If you create a reusable presenter or voice from someone's likeness or speech, we also record the applicable source authorization and identity verification state. The portrait-and-narration Standard flow records authorization for the specific photo and recording used in that render. Where the separate subject capture flow is available, it can also collect a separate consent recording and the subject's acknowledgments. Saving that recording does not mean an identity is approved.

Payment information. Purchases are handled directly by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number — only what Stripe returns to confirm a purchase (amount, credits granted, transaction status).

Technical information. A session cookie that keeps you signed in, and standard operational logs (timestamps, error reports) used to keep the service running and to diagnose problems. Error reports are scrubbed of session tokens and authorization headers before they ever leave our systems.

We do not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics on this site.

How we use your information

  • To create and verify an authorized presenter or voice where that feature is available, render the video you request, and let you review, download, and manage it.
  • To operate your account: sign-in, credit balance, purchase history, and support requests.
  • To detect and prevent abuse, and to keep the service secure and reliable.
  • To send you service email: render-ready notifications, sign-in links, and purchase receipts.

We do not sell your information. Authorized source media may be processed to create a reusable presenter or voice for your account and to generate the videos you request.

Who we share information with

We use a small number of service providers to operate LUX, each of which only receives what it needs to do its job:

  • Google — if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Resend — to deliver sign-in and notification email.
  • Stripe — to process credit-pack purchases.
  • HeyGen — for supported scripted Standard and Premium videos and, where enabled, creation and verification of reusable presenters and voices. Depending on the feature you use, this can include an authorized photo or recording, presenter description, script, presenter and voice identifiers, and the resulting video. A separate subject consent recording would be sent only if the applicable verification path is enabled.
  • RunPod — when selected for the portrait-and-narration Standard rendering path, to process the uploaded portrait and narration for that video.
  • Vercel and Neon — our hosting, storage, and database infrastructure.
  • Sentry — sanitized error monitoring, with authentication data removed before we receive it.

We do not share your information with any other party for their own marketing or advertising purposes.

Authorization for a person's likeness and voice

The portrait-and-narration Standard flow asks you to confirm your authority to use the specific photo and recording for that render. A reusable presenter or voice based on a real person requires that person's authorization for the source media and permitted uses. A provider may have additional verification requirements. A photo or prompt avatar with no provider consent API step still requires the person's permission when it uses or imitates a real person's likeness. You can request withdrawal of a reusable identity by contacting us.

Data retention and deletion

Account content and finished videos may remain available while your account is active. Temporary uploads and separate verification recordings have their own expiry and cleanup process; withdrawal or deletion requests may also require us to remove provider-held identity assets. To request deletion of your account and associated data, or withdrawal of a reusable presenter or voice, email ariel@luxmarketingcompany.com — we don't currently have a self-service delete button, so this goes to a real person.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain uses of it. Contact us at the address above and we'll act on it; we may need to verify it's really your account first.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards — encrypted connections, access-controlled storage, and least-privilege access to production systems — but no service can guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password (or sign in with Google) and let us know right away if you notice anything suspicious on your account.

Children's privacy

LUX Video OS is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, let account holders know by email.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email ariel@luxmarketingcompany.com.
