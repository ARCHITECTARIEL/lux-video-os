FAQ

Questions people actually ask

What can I make with LUX Video OS?

Create scripted presenter videos that introduce your business, explain a service, promote an offer, announce something new, or answer a customer question.

Which presenter and voice can I use?

The current workflow uses a ready enrolled presenter and reusable voice already available in your workspace. Additional presenter and voice choices are not available in this release.

Can LUX help me write the script?

An optional AI Copywriter may be available separately in your workspace. It is separate from the render form, and you review every draft before using it.

How do credits work?

Rendering requires credits; no complimentary render or automatic trial credits are included. Regular new accounts start with zero render credits. Studio shows a server-issued quote before you confirm a render.

Can I download completed videos?

Completed, accepted videos are available in your account for download. Creating a video sends the inputs needed for that workflow to configured service providers under their applicable terms. Review the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service before use.

How are my enrolled presenter and voice used?

LUX asks for workflow-specific permission before processing. A reusable enrolled presenter and voice may be used for later scripts you authorize until you revoke them; legacy narration workflows may use render-specific inputs.

How long does a render take?

Rendering is asynchronous — you'll see live status while it processes, and can come back to download it once it's ready.

What happens to unused credits?

Studio shows your current credit balance and the server-issued quote for each render before confirmation.
