FAQ

Questions people actually ask

What can I make with LUX Video OS?

Create videos that introduce your business, explain a service, promote an offer, announce something new, or answer a customer question.

Do I have to appear in the video?

No. You can use your own photo and voice, or choose an AI presenter to deliver your message.

Can LUX help me write the script?

Yes. The script-based workflow includes AI writing help. Review and adjust the draft so it accurately reflects your business, your offer, and your voice.

What is included for free?

Your new account includes enough credits to create one video, so you can try the process and see the result before buying more credits.

Do I own the videos I create?

Yes. Every finished render is stored privately to your account and is yours to download. Nothing is shared, sold, or used to train other models.

Is my photo or voice used for anything besides my own video?

No. Standard-tier renders record your explicit consent for that specific portrait and narration, tied to that render only.

How long does a render take?

Rendering is asynchronous — you'll see live status while it processes, and can come back to download it once it's ready.

What happens to unused credits?

They stay on your account and roll forward — credits are only spent when you actually submit a render.
